title: Can I get a refund?
badge: refund
---
Yes. Request a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase; no reason is required. Email order support with your order reference and purchase date using the address on the [refund policy](/terms/#refunds). Refunds are reviewed manually and returned to the original payment method. Do not post order details or payment credentials in a public GitHub issue.
